# programs

All FAUST programs of the module. `../firmware` builds them into one
firmware for rev2 (encoder to choose), `../single` builds one of them alone
(rev1, or to try a program). Parameters bind to the hardware by their label
metadata (`[hw:..]`, `[rev1:..]`, `[rev2:..]`, `[cv:..]`, see
`../lib/nemesis/src/nemesis_program.cpp`); on rev2 the parameters without a
knob are settings (turn the encoder). Programs that follow the tempo use the
system transport with `import("nemesis.lib");` (`../faust/nemesis.lib`).
The comment at the top of each `.dsp` has the details.

| program | what | rev2 controls | rev1 controls |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |

| `superosc.dsp` | stereo drone: root/fourth/fifth as detuned saw partials, ladder filter, limiter; MIDI note + V/Oct; out 3/4 pitch CV and gate (MIDI to CV) | X tune, Y cutoff, Z resonance, CV A V/Oct, POT A–D root, fourth, fifth, detune; octave, drift (CC 1), gated, swell, level as settings | CV X/Y/Z, A–D, V/Oct on CV E |
| `growler.dsp` | stereo 4-operator FM bass around cross modulation, the operators played by keys or V/Oct (keys: spread — the notes played together share the operators: 1 note all four, 2 notes 1+2 / 3+4, 3 notes 1+2 / 3 / 4, 4 notes one each, the lowest first, lifting keeps it; round robin; or held), v-oct 1–4 (CV A–D as assigned) transpose and offset them; a knob per operator (depth, or level and depth as a carrier), carrier and operator 4 modulating each other (cross), feedback, 16 ratio sets, 7 algorithms (parallel: all four operators as carriers, a chord from four keys), wavefolder, ladder filter with a decay envelope, left/right detuned by width, clean sub; vca off for a drone; out 3/4 filter and amp envelope CVs | X/Y/Z mod 2/3/4, POT A–D cross, feedback, ratios, cutoff; CV targets v-oct 1–4, cv gate (default CV A → v-oct 1, CV B → gate); mode, keys, vca, width, drive, resonance, env filter, filter decay, sub, octave, glide, attack, release, velocity, level as settings | CV X/Y/Z, A–D, V/Oct on CV E, gate on CV F |
| `pollen.dsp` | live granular sampler (prototype): a looping live memory per channel (2/4/8/10 s, freeze holds it, CV gate assignable), 8 grain voices that keep start/size/pitch/direction/pan from their start, window shapes, density (overlap) or synced note values, scan, spray, reverse share, pitch spray + quantize, pan spread, tone, blur (4-line FDN); out 3 grain trigger, 4 grain envelope, 5 random per grain, 6 pitch (1 V/oct), 7/8 wet | X position, Y size, Z pitch; POT A–D density, spray, blur, mix; freeze, memory, sync, scan, reverse, pitch spray, quantize, shape, pan spread, tone, blur input (IN 1/2 into the blur, heard also with mix dry) as settings | CV X Y Z, CV A B C F |
| `strike.dsp` | six voice physical modelling percussion: mallet (stiffness, dynamics: loud hits brighter) + noise burst, noise blend between them (attack, decay, density, lp/hp) into modal resonators (string, beam, marimba, drumhead, membrane, plate) with decay, strike position, tone (material), inharmonicity, low cut, bend (pitch envelope on the 2 lowest modes), tension (loud hits higher, relaxing), variation (every hit a little different); noise out, rattle (wires), level, pan, choke groups A/B; random sounds: roll the selected voice or all (history of 16, turn back to undo; button 1 held + button 2 rolls and plays the voice), amount; 4-6 modes per voice slot; triggers on IN 1-6 and MIDI (setting midi: channels = voice 1-6 on channels base..base+5, any note, C4 = its pitch, velocity; or notes 36-41 on the base channel); out 1/2 stereo mix, 3-8 the voices | X/Y/Z (knob + CV) assignable for all voices (settings x, y, z: decay, hardness, drive, pitch, tone, noise, position, bend, tension, variation, rattle; default decay/hardness/drive); POT A-D inharmonic, position, bend, tension of the selected voice (button 1 held: all voices); button 1 next voice, button 2 plays it; LEDs: selected / any voice; settings: the selected voice's parameters in sections object, exciter, output, then random (target, roll, amount: rolls leave the pot settings and CV-played settings alone) and kit (midi, x, y, z) | CV X Y Z |
| `breaker.dsp` | beat slice repeat/reverse, crush, comb, drive on the transport (MIDI clock or tempo); outs 3–8 drum triggers (kick, snare, closed/open hh, perc 1/2) from a 4×4 morphing pattern map | X slice, Y rev-rep chance (centre off), Z bipolar comb, CV A/B gates, POT A/B crush, drive, POT C/D (+ CV C/D) pattern x/y; mix, density, swing, trig length as settings | written for rev2 |

`sketches/` holds unfinished programs (bell, mono_voice, polySynth); they
are not built. Copy one up here and add it to a firmware to try it.
