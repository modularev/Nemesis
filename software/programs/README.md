# programs

All FAUST programs of the module. `../firmware` builds them into one
firmware for rev2 (encoder to choose), `../single` builds one of them alone
(rev1, or to try a program). Parameters bind to the hardware by their label
metadata (`[hw:..]`, `[rev1:..]`, `[rev2:..]`, `[cv:..]`, see
`../lib/nemesis/src/nemesis_program.cpp`); on rev2 the parameters without a
knob are settings (turn the encoder). Programs that follow the tempo use the
system transport with `import("nemesis.lib");` (`../faust/nemesis.lib`).
The comment at the top of each `.dsp` has the details.

| program | what | rev2 controls | rev1 controls |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `juno.dsp` | 6 voice MIDI polysynth with chorus, stereo out 1/2 | POT A–D cutoff, resonance, env, release; X/Y/Z LFO rate, PWM, chorus; envelope and mixer as settings and MIDI CC | knobs A–D, X/Y/Z |
| `mooSpace.dsp` | stereo reverb, in/out 1/2 | X/Y/Z smear, decay, drag; POT A–D filter, modulate, colour, mix (+ CV A–D); lag, push as settings | CV X–Z, A–F (smear decay drag filter modulate colour lag push mix) |
| `mooSpace2.dsp` | mooSpace v1's algorithm with wider controls: smear = how much it blurs (strength of all diffusers: clear separate reflections to dense; eight input diffusers (four fixed) at most 0.6, so at full smear a transient fades in over ~60 ms instead of clicking through), drag = how far it stretches (diffuser lengths 0.05–9.5x and late taps up to 2x v1), shimmer (pitch shifter +12/+7/+5/+19/−12 where input and feedback enter the tank: an octave reverb at short decays, stacking octaves at long ones), modulation as a setting, tone in the tank (dark: damping + input down to 400 Hz with make-up gain; thin: high-passes in input and loops up to 2.5 kHz), reverse (forward to reversed pieces of 1/3 s into the tank), decay sets the tail time (0.4–60 s exponential up to 0.95, endless above), soft limiters instead of the tank compressors (no pumping), an extra allpass per tank half, limiter; sync setting: with the transport's tempo lag snaps to 1/16 steps, drag's main late tap to 1/32 steps and the reverse pieces are a note value (reverse length 1/16, 1/8, 1/8., 1/4, 1/2; on the beat while the transport runs), the tank delays stay free; motion setting: tank and input diffuser delays glide (pitch) or crossfade (no pitch; lag still glides) ; freeze (setting/CV gate: input out, loop gain 1, tail held), echoes + echo time (ping-pong taps 1–4 note values from the reverse buffers into the tank), duck (wet down while the input plays), gate (wet chopped on the transport grid), lofi (sample rate/bit reduction in the tank feedback, 48 kHz/16 bit .. 4 kHz/6 bit); out 1/2 mix, 3/4 rear (wet tap differences, quad with 1/2), 5/6 wet only, 7 tail envelope, 8 input envelope (CV 0–5 V) | X smear, Y decay, Z drag; POT A–D reverse, shimmer, tone, mix; sync, reverse length, freeze, echoes, echo time, duck, gate, lofi, modulate (CV assignable), shift, lag (ms, or a note value with sync), push, motion (on: glide, off: 25 ms crossfades) as settings | as mooSpace |
| `freqshift.dsp` | stereo frequency shifter with feedback delay; out 1/2 main, 3/4 up, 5/6 down, 7/8 quadrature oscillator | X shift, Y range (10 Hz–4 kHz), Z stereo spread; POT A–D mix, delay, feedback, sideband (+ CV A–D); damp as setting | CV X/Y/Z, A–D |
| `superosc.dsp` | stereo drone: root/fourth/fifth as detuned saw partials, ladder filter, limiter; MIDI note + V/Oct; out 3/4 pitch CV and gate (MIDI to CV) | X tune, Y cutoff, Z resonance, CV A V/Oct, POT A–D root, fourth, fifth, detune; octave, drift (CC 1), gated, swell, level as settings | CV X/Y/Z, A–D, V/Oct on CV E |
| `growler.dsp` | stereo 4-operator FM bass around cross modulation, the operators played by keys or V/Oct (keys: spread — the notes played together share the operators: 1 note all four, 2 notes 1+2 / 3+4, 3 notes 1+2 / 3 / 4, 4 notes one each, the lowest first, lifting keeps it; round robin; or held), v-oct 1–4 (CV A–D as assigned) transpose and offset them; a knob per operator (depth, or level and depth as a carrier), carrier and operator 4 modulating each other (cross), feedback, 16 ratio sets, 7 algorithms (parallel: all four operators as carriers, a chord from four keys), wavefolder, ladder filter with a decay envelope, left/right detuned by width, clean sub; vca off for a drone; out 3/4 filter and amp envelope CVs | X/Y/Z mod 2/3/4, POT A–D cross, feedback, ratios, cutoff; CV targets v-oct 1–4, cv gate (default CV A → v-oct 1, CV B → gate); mode, keys, vca, width, drive, resonance, env filter, filter decay, sub, octave, glide, attack, release, velocity, level as settings | CV X/Y/Z, A–D, V/Oct on CV E, gate on CV F |
| `breaker.dsp` | beat slice repeat/reverse, crush, comb, drive on the transport (MIDI clock or tempo); outs 3–8 drum triggers (kick, snare, closed/open hh, perc 1/2) from a 4×4 morphing pattern map | X slice, Y rev-rep chance (centre off), Z bipolar comb, CV A/B gates, POT A/B crush, drive, POT C/D (+ CV C/D) pattern x/y; mix, density, swing, trig length as settings | written for rev2 |

`sketches/` holds unfinished programs (bell, mono_voice, polySynth); they
are not built. Copy one up here and add it to a firmware to try it.
